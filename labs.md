# Labs and organisations

Who is working on general AI, and where to follow their work. The list covers labs that train frontier models, groups that measure them, and academic and non-profit groups whose research the field depends on. Last checked on 16 October 2026. If a lab is missing or a detail has changed, please open an issue with a source.

## Measurement and evaluation

These groups produce most of the numbers on the [tracker](tracker.md).

| Organisation | Founded | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| [METR](https://metr.org) | 2023 (began as ARC Evals) | Time horizons of AI agents on long tasks (T3), and dangerous capabilities |
| [Epoch AI](https://epoch.ai) | 2022 | Compute, data and cost trends, the Epoch Capabilities Index, FrontierMath |
| [ARC Prize Foundation](https://arcprize.org) | 2024 | ARC-AGI benchmarks for learning new skills (T2) |
| [Center for AI Safety](https://safe.ai) | 2022 | Humanity's Last Exam (with Scale AI), safety research |
| [UK AI Security Institute](https://www.aisi.gov.uk) | 2023 | Government testing of frontier models before release |
| [US Center for AI Standards and Innovation](https://www.nist.gov/caisi) | 2023 (as the US AI Safety Institute) | Government testing and standards, part of NIST |
