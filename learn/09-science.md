# AI for maths and science

By the end of this module you should be able to describe how AI went from passing exams to solving open problems, and why AI that does AI research matters. Producing new knowledge sits at the top of T1, and if AI speeds up AI research itself, every timeline gets shorter.

## Main ideas

Maths benchmarks have climbed from school problems to olympiad problems (gold-medal level at the IMO in 2025), then to research-level problems in FrontierMath tiers 1 to 4, and in 2026 to open problems in FrontierMath: Erdős.

Much of this work pairs a generator with a verifier. A language model proposes an answer, and a checker such as the Lean proof assistant or a set of code tests confirms it. AlphaEvolve adds evolutionary search on top. AlphaFold, which predicts protein structures, shared the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.

RE-Bench, MLE-bench and PaperBench test whether agents can do machine-learning research. Since October 2026, METR has treated acceleration of AI research as a main risk signal.
