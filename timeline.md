# Timeline

Events that changed what researchers thought AI could do, or how they measured it. The "Test" column links each event to the [five tests](definition.md).

## Foundations, 1950 to 2016

| Date | Event | Significance | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1950 | Turing proposes the imitation game | The first behavioural test for machine intelligence | Turing, *Mind* 1950 |
| 1956 | Dartmouth workshop | AI is founded as a field | McCarthy et al. proposal |
| 1997 | Deep Blue beats Kasparov at chess | Superhuman performance in one narrow domain | IBM |
| 2007 | Legg and Hutter define universal intelligence | A formal definition of general intelligence | [0712.3329](https://arxiv.org/abs/0712.3329) |
| 2012 | AlexNet wins ImageNet | The deep-learning era begins | Krizhevsky et al., NeurIPS 2012 |
| 2016 | AlphaGo beats Lee Sedol | Learning combined with search beats humans at a game thought to need intuition | Silver et al., *Nature* 2016 |
