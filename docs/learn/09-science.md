# 09 · AI for maths and science

**Goal.** You can describe how AI went from passing exams to solving open problems, and why AI doing AI research matters.

**Why it matters for AGI.** Producing *new* knowledge is the top of T1. If AI speeds up AI research itself, timelines shorten.

## Core ideas

- **Benchmark ladder:** school maths → olympiad (IMO gold-medal level, 2025) → research problems (FrontierMath Tiers 1–4) → open problems (FrontierMath: Erdős, 2026).
- **Generate and verify:** a language model proposes and a checker verifies (a proof assistant such as Lean, or code tests). AlphaEvolve adds evolutionary search.
- **Scientific tools:** AlphaFold (protein structure, Nobel Prize 2024).
- **Automated AI R&D:** benchmarks such as RE-Bench, MLE-bench and PaperBench measure whether agents can do ML research. METR now treats AI-R&D acceleration as a key risk signal (Oct 2026).
