# Timeline: milestones on the road to AGI

Only events that changed what the field believed AI could do, or how it measures that. Each row has a source. "AGI test" links the event to [What is AGI?](definition.md).

## Foundations (1950–2016)

| Date | Event | Why it mattered | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1950 | Turing proposes the imitation game | First behavioural test for machine intelligence | Turing, *Mind* 1950 |
| 1956 | Dartmouth workshop | Founding of AI as a field | McCarthy et al. proposal |
| 1997 | Deep Blue beats Kasparov at chess | Narrow superhuman AI | IBM |
| 2007 | Legg & Hutter define universal intelligence | The formal "general" definition | [0712.3329](https://arxiv.org/abs/0712.3329) |
| 2012 | AlexNet wins ImageNet | Start of the deep-learning era | Krizhevsky et al., NeurIPS 2012 |
| 2016 | AlphaGo beats Lee Sedol | Learning plus search beats humans at a game thought to need intuition | Silver et al., *Nature* 2016 |
